# Optional visual treatment: sticker

The `sticker` implementation in `assets/lecture-template/src/theme/sticker.tsx` provides rounded white cards, soft outlines, pastel accents, and paper-like decoration. It can support informal, friendly explanations or audience-facing stories, but can feel visually busy or juvenile for some subjects. This is a choice, not the identity every video must adopt.

## Use when it helps

A tape strip, tilted label, doodle, or sticker edge may distinguish an annotation or make ownership visible. Use only those that communicate something. Keep text and important data live in Remotion, use high-contrast text colors, and leave a quiet area behind captions and diagrams. A still card or unadorned equation is acceptable.

The former per-canvas background bitmap placed a mint grid and corner doodles behind open process areas. The bundled eight-shot sample was reviewed at 390px in all three aspect ratios; the grid added visual texture beneath the process flow without helping explain it. The template now uses a quiet pale-mint field while retaining the white sticker edges, washi tape and annotation accents. The old `public/bg-sticker-*` files are retained as inactive source assets, not loaded by this theme. Do not add marks merely to fill space.

## Boundaries

Use the template's pastel treatment coherently rather than mixing unrelated accents. Keep labels readable at output size. Tape, white sticker outlines, shadow, tilt, and rounded corners are not requirements. Movement is warranted only when a change is meaningful; do not animate a stationary label or add sound to every entrance.
