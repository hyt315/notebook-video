# Storyboard: GitHub Open-Source Trilogy EP0 (template example)

`paper` treatment · 16:9 · 1,150 frames (38.3 s) · 4 chapters · 8 shots

This is an editable teaching example, not a requirement to copy its subject matter, layouts, components, or animation. Its camera is static and its generic shot entrances/reveals are off; individual scene elements still change where a visible process or comparison helps explain the narration.

The authoritative declarations are in `manifests/shots.json`. Frame ranges are generated by `scripts/resolve-shots.py`; do not hand-edit generated frame numbers. Each narration cue must map to exactly one shot, but a shot may carry several cues with one coherent visual. The optional `beats` entries mark authored timing points; they do not imply that every cue needs a new object or animation.

## Review notes

- Judge each carrier against the actual narrated cue in the rendered film; a declaration is not proof that the picture is clear or factually correct.
- Check the complete film with sound, not only stills or contact sheets. Confirm that any local reveal/highlight coincides with the spoken idea and that the shot leaves time to read.
- Inspect representative frames at 390 CSS pixels as a practical mobile review viewport, not as a universal pass threshold.
- The example retains its original 16:9 layout and has separate 4:3 and 3:4 reflows. All three were fully rendered and reviewed at 390px for this update; this does not automatically validate newly authored projects. Audio, TTS, synchronization, and learning outcomes were not reviewed here.
